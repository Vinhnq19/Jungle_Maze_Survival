Pixel standard (same as the 32 px restyle):
- Colored icons: drawn on a 32×32 native grid, exported 64×64 with nearest ×2; binary alpha; 1 px outline `#0C1417`; only colours from `Tools/Art/pixel_palette.json`, at most 8 per icon; content about 56/64 px, centred within 1 px, at least 3 px transparent padding on every edge.
- Masks: pure white RGB 255 wherever alpha > 0, binary alpha, so `Image` tint decides the colour.
- New, original drawings only. Do not copy sprites from `Assets/Imported/JSurviv` or any other game.
